# Academy Learning Path

The Academy uses one repeatable loop: **Learn → Practice → Simulate → Compete**. Competition is optional; professional evidence comes from qualifying case behavior rather than participation rewards.

| Phase | Objectives | Game mechanics and practice | Simulation and competition | Evidence, skills, and career relevance | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 — Orientation | Understand cases, evidence, decisions, Kareem, progression, and Skill Passport | Two-minute profile setup and a guided first mission | Introduction to Mizan Trading | No competence claim from onboarding; prepares the player to collect valid evidence | Implemented foundation |
| 1 — Accounting Foundations | Equation, account types, Chart of Accounts, Nature of Accounts, debit/credit, source documents, journal/ledger/trial-balance concepts | Existing Accounting Equation lessons, 197-account reference, classification, Money Flow, Journal Practice | Short business examples and optional accuracy challenges | Fundamentals, classification, debit/credit, and document-analysis practice; relevant to every entry role | Implemented integration |
| 2 — Transaction Practice | Purchases, sales, receipts, payments, expenses, assets, basic adjustments | Journal repair, account/side selection, document inspection | Progressive Mizan Trading work ending in First Shift | Case events can support journal, AP, AR, document, supplier, cash, and expense evidence | Implemented initial content |
| 3 — Department Accounting | AP, AR, Cash & Bank, Inventory, Expenses & Assets | Existing Missions, Detective, Money Flow, and journal mechanics | Department zones and future department boss cases | Builds role-specific coverage for AP, AR, Treasury, Inventory, and General Accounting | Structure/preview |
| 4 — Period End / Closing | Bank reconciliation, accruals, prepayments, adjustments, trial balance, controlled close | Future reconciliation and workpaper mechanics | Closing Week / Month-End Crisis | Intended for GL and General Accountant evidence | Locked; Chapter 2 not implemented |
| 5 — Real Business Tracks | Apply accounting in Trading, Restaurant, Retail, Pharmacy, Contracting, and Manufacturing contexts | Shared Case Engine with sector metadata | Future business-specific simulations | Intended to add context and complexity without inventing completion | Metadata/planned |
| 6 — Career Readiness | Interpret Skill Passport, choose a target role, close skill gaps, build CV, understand employer evidence | Role selection, weighted coverage, missing-skill recommendation | Career Hub and employer preview | Profile and CV update from qualifying evidence; local evidence remains non-Verified | Implemented foundation |

## Progression rules

- Essential foundations remain required even when onboarding experience is `Experienced Accountant`.
- Game Level and XP control game progression and rewards only.
- Professional rank and role readiness come from Skill Passport coverage.
- First Shift stays intact and is integrated as the first professional shift after the foundations.
- Phase 4 and Month End remain locked until their real content and progression rules exist.
- Placement architecture returns a recommended Phase 0, 1, or 2 starting point; it does not skip foundations or grant evidence.
