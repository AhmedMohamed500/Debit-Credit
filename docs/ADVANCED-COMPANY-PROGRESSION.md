# Advanced Company Progression

The five Career League companies represent increasing responsibility rather than cosmetic themes.

| Tier | Company | Responsibility | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mizan Trading | Understand and process | Implemented entry world |
| 2 | Delta Commerce | Own structured processes | Architecture ready |
| 3 | Horizon Industries | Close and control | Architecture ready |
| 4 | Orbit Regional Group | Review and report | Planned |
| 5 | Atlas Global Simulation | Corporate/global simulation | Planned |

Each tier increases process complexity, ambiguity, control depth, English documentation, review responsibility and deadline pressure. Unlocks continue to depend on missions, evidence and promotion assessments rather than XP.

The current status labels are product boundaries: architecture-ready tiers have data contracts and unlock rules but still need complete playable case sets; planned tiers cannot grant evidence.
