# Career League Curriculum

| Stage | Learn when needed | Practice | Simulate | Compete / Boss | Evidence | Career relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Foundations | Equation, types, debit/credit | Move money, classify effects | Small business transactions | Foundation challenge | Practice events | Student confidence |
| Bookkeeping | Journal, ledger, trial balance | Build and repair entries | Mizan Trading books | Ledger Rescue | Accuracy and judgment | Junior Accountant |
| First company work | Documents, AP/AR, cash/bank | Inspect, decide, post | First Shift | First Real Shift | Investigation, risk, independence | Entry roles |
| Structured cycles | Purchase/sales cycle, reconciliation | Match and reconcile | Medium-company queues | Bank/Supplier Crisis | Demonstrated workflow evidence | AP, AR, Treasury, General |
| Corporate close | Accruals, prepayments, cut-off, controls | Close checklist and review | Corporate month-end | Month-End Crisis / CFO Review | Connected-case evidence | General Accountant / GL |
| Corporate simulation | ERP concepts, audit, reporting, communication | Cross-team decisions | 90 Days in Corporate | Promotion Assessment | Stronger professional evidence | Larger-company readiness |

The product presents a business problem first. Reference knowledge appears when the player needs it; the player then investigates, decides, sees consequences, and creates evidence. Career League scoring favors accuracy, judgment, investigation, risk awareness, first-attempt quality, difficulty, boss completion, and consistency. Speed has low/no direct weight.
