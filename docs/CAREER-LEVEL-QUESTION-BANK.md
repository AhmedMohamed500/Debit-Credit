# Career-Level Accounting Case Bank

This repository now contains the initial reviewed case bank for the Career Content Engine. It is a case bank rather than a generic quiz bank.

| Level | Case | Work product | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | The Owner Opens the Shop | Visual accounting equation | Understand |
| 1 | First Supported Entry | Receipt-to-journal file | Record |

## Review boundaries

- **Review Required:** Material or Merely Different?, Audit Request Board, Growth Without Cash.
- **Draft:** Competing Capex Requests.

These cases exist so the architecture and curriculum are visible, but they are excluded from the approved playable selector. Approval requires accounting treatment, business realism, and Arabic/English editorial review.

## Expansion standard

Each new topic should add multiple business variants, different evidence quality, and consequences. A higher career level must add ownership, ambiguity, review, control, or decision responsibility. Merely increasing amounts or arithmetic does not qualify.
