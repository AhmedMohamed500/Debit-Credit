# Professional Certification Preparation Roadmap

Future tracks are planned for CMA preparation, ACCA-related readiness, DipIFR preparation and CPA-style knowledge practice.

Every track is explicitly a **Preparation Track**, **Readiness Track** or **Practice Track**. Debit & Credit does not claim that these are official courses, accredited certifications, official partnerships or exam authorization.

The tracks remain locked and planned until reviewed learning outcomes, source references, question licensing boundaries and content maintenance ownership are defined. They cannot create professional assessment evidence in the current frontend-only release.
