# Complete Accounting Career Curriculum

This is the master curriculum reference for Debit & Credit. It maps accounting knowledge to simulated professional responsibility. A status of Implemented means playable code exists now. Foundation means the reusable engine or data contract exists, while full case coverage is still incomplete. Planned means the module is a product commitment, not a current feature. Review required means no professional assessment or Demonstrated claim may be produced until an accountable subject-matter review is recorded.

## Progression standard

The canonical responsibility ladder is Understand → Record → Process → Reconcile → Review → Control → Analyze → Manage → Decide. Levels are simulation levels: L0 Beginner, L1 Student, L2 Graduate/Trainee, L3 Junior, L4 Functional Accountant, L5 GL Accountant, L6 Senior, L7 Chief/Supervisor, and L8 Finance Manager. They do not grant real job titles.

Evidence uses only Practiced and Demonstrated for local play. Verified is reserved for a future independently controlled assessment repository. CV behavior means an evidence-gated simulated-work bullet may be generated; XP, Coins, persona choice, placement and browsing never create a CV claim.

## Responsibility and content controls

- A real person at the stated level must reasonably face the responsibility.
- Higher levels add ownership, ambiguity, controls, review, analysis and decision impact rather than harder arithmetic alone.
- Every scored case must declare skill impact, evidence rule, role relevance, CV behavior, content status and review state.
- Draft, preview, planned and review-required content cannot create Demonstrated evidence or enter competition scoring.
- Competition compares the same challenge seed and version inside a fair career/persona segment. Local/offline results remain editable and unverified.
- A failed or unsafe action is an event and consequence, never positive professional evidence. Repeated attempts cannot farm evidence or rewards.
- Existing evidence is not destructively downgraded. New projections remain deterministic and backward compatible.

## Delivery boundary

The current release provides the Bootcamp, Account City, First Shift/Gameplay Phase B, persona placement and diagnostic engines, professional content catalog and filtering, Mizan progression, connected mission consequences, local multi-profile competition contracts, Player Profile projection, Skill Passport/Career Gap integration, ATS CV and local revisions, Corporate Bridge architecture with initial approved weeks, Month-End orchestration contracts, company-tier progression, and roadmap contracts for standards and certifications.

Full playable case libraries for all levels, remote competition, identity verification, employer accounts, cross-device sync, advanced company worlds, professional standards assessment and certification preparation remain future work. These boundaries are product states, not hidden unlocks.
