# Corporate Bridge Track

## Small Company → Structured Medium Company

Required practice: reliable journals, source-document review, AP and AR basics, cash/bank handling, manager escalation, and Promotion Assessment 1. The workflow shifts from direct posting to queues, approval, matching, review status, and subledger awareness.

## Structured Medium Company → Corporate

Required practice: bank and supplier/customer reconciliation, three-way match, accruals, prepayments, fixed assets basics, cut-off, month-end checklist, controls, and reporting deadlines. Advancement depends on Skill Passport coverage, qualifying missions, professional performance, and assessment—not XP.

## Corporate → Regional / Multinational-style simulation

Planned skills: English documentation, audit requests, close calendar, intercompany concepts, ERP-style workflow, role specialization, professional communication, and high-pressure connected cases. These tiers remain fictional simulations and never claim employment experience.

## 90 Days in Corporate Accounting

- Week 1: documents, queues, approvals, and workflow differences — vertical-slice architecture implemented.
- Week 2: structured AP/AR — planned.
- Week 3: bank and reconciliations — planned.
- Week 4: close, cut-off, accruals and prepayments — planned.
- Later: controls, audit and reporting — planned.
