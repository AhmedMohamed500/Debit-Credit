# Small Company → Corporate Bridge

## Positioning

Corporate Bridge is a simulation of the first 90 days in structured accounting for people moving from small-company work. It does not claim employment, placement or certification.

## Current approved slice

**Week 1 — Structured AP Intake** introduces an invoice intake log, approval chain and three-way matching file. The player validates vendor, PO, goods receipt and approval before posting.

**Week 2 — Supplier and Customer Reconciliations** introduces supplier statement reconciliation and an unapplied-cash list. The player investigates differences and assigns documented ownership.

## Planned locked weeks

- Week 3: accruals, prepayments and fixed assets.
- Week 4: cut-off and close controls.
- Week 5: reporting, audit requests and ERP-style workflow.

Planned weeks remain non-playable. Completing approved local work cannot bypass the content review boundary.

## Progression

Weeks unlock through completed work products and prerequisites rather than XP. Corporate Bridge continues the existing event → performance → evidence → Skill Passport → CV pipeline when a case is approved for professional evidence.
