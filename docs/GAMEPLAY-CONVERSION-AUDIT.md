# Gameplay conversion audit

This audit distinguishes work simulation from educational checks; it does not delete the latter. “Real gameplay” means a source-driven workplace action changes simulated state and produces a consequence. “Semi-interactive” means useful practice or navigation without a complete professional decision loop. “Academic question” means an answer/check prompt rather than an assigned accounting file.

| --- | --- | --- |
| First Shift supplier invoice, customer receipt and office expense at `/game/first-shift` | Real gameplay | Extend the shared workspace to discrepancy cases; retain safe posting. |
| Bank Reconciliation workpaper at `/game/bank-reconciliation` | Real gameplay | Add a bank notice to the work inbox only after reusing its current reconciliation validator and evidence rule. |
| Promotion Assessment / Corporate Bridge | Real gameplay / semi-interactive by subtask | Turn remaining short checks into reviewer workpapers with source evidence and sign-off authority. |
| Career League compact daily challenges, sealed duels and leaderboard | Semi-interactive game practice | Keep competition rewards separate; route professional proof to full approved cases, not wins. |
| Account City, account guide, money-flow and financial-statement explorers | Semi-interactive learning | Embed as optional tools inside a case; do not invent ledger side effects. |
| Journal-entry practice, mission question banks and standard practice routes | Academic question / guided practice | Present a transaction file, source references, workpaper, decision and consequence for professional-mode variants; keep short questions for concept checks. |
| Academy lessons, bootcamp checkpoints, detective learning prompts | Academic question / semi-interactive learning | Keep knowledge checks; convert only claimed job-ready assessments into multi-document investigation. |

| Skill Passport, Career Gap, Career Profile, ATS CV | Derived career surfaces, not gameplay | Continue to consume qualified evidence; never let a player self-award verified skill from quiz or XP. |
| Landing page, companies and jobs showcases | Navigation/marketing, not gameplay | Keep truthful route and maturity labels. |
