# Personal Career Map

`/{locale}/career-league/map` is a role-specific sequence calculated from `CareerLeagueState.targetRoleId`, the role catalog and the saved Skill Passport projection. A General Accountant path explicitly covers journal entries, bank reconciliation, adjusting entries, expense recognition/prepayment concepts, trial balance, month-end and financial statements. Other targets use their weighted role-skill requirements.

Each node shows its skill, a reason the skill matters, related accounting roles, a status and a required activity. Statuses:

- **Demonstrated / Practiced:** only from saved gameplay evidence.
- **Available:** the next unmet skill with a currently playable route.
- **Locked:** another skill with a route that follows earlier progress; its activity can be inspected.
- **Planned:** its training activity is not yet playable, so no launch link is shown.

Verified is not generated by the map or local diagnostic. The destination role is explicitly a **target**, not an awarded title. The first available activity is the next mission; a planned node does not falsely block every other playable activity. The map links back to Career Profile, Skill Passport, role-based Career Gap, ATS CV, Bootcamp, Mizan Trading and Career League.

An explicit Promotion Assessment checkpoint appears before the target. Its link opens the existing early assessment requirements; higher-role assessments are still planned. Simply viewing the map does not satisfy eligibility or grant a promotion.

The map and gap are read-only projections; they do not mutate saved evidence or complete missions on page load. Existing local saves are migrated conservatively. The future backend can consume versioned state and evidence separately without conflating self-report, diagnostic judgment and demonstrated gameplay.
