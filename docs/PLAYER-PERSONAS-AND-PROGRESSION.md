# Player Personas and Progression

## Accounting Student

- **Starting point:** foundations or an optional placement check.
- **Needs:** account types, debit/credit, transaction effects, journal, ledger, trial balance.
- **Journey:** Foundations → Nature of Accounts → Journal → Ledger → Trial Balance → First Shift → Company Work.
- **Goals:** confidence, career discovery, first accounting job.
- **Recommended missions:** classification, journal repair, ledger rescue, First Shift.
- **Outcome:** evidence-backed readiness for entry company work.

## Fresh Graduate

- **Starting point:** Placement Challenge and a practical skill-gap view.
- **Needs:** documents, posting, AP/AR, bank, reconciliation, adjustments, closing.
- **Journey:** Placement → Document Handling → AP/AR → Bank → Reconciliation → Close → Interview/Promotion Assessment.
- **Goals:** first role, General Accountant, AP, AR, Treasury, GL.
- **Recommended missions:** First Shift, supplier/customer work, bank and trial-balance cases.
- **Outcome:** converts theory into visible simulation evidence and role readiness.

## Working Accountant

- **Starting point:** current environment, career goal, Placement Challenge.
- **Needs:** structured cycles, controls, reconciliations, month-end, reporting, ERP-style workflow and professional communication.
- **Journey:** Gap Report → Corporate Bridge → structured AP/AR → reconciliation → controls → closing → promotion assessment → bigger simulated company.
- **Goals:** larger company, corporate accounting, General Accountant or a specialist track.
- **Recommended missions:** Corporate Bridge, bank reconciliation, closing, audit and reporting cases as they become available.
- **Outcome:** keeps real work history separate while adding clearly labeled simulation-based corporate practice.

All personas converge on the same gameplay → event → evidence → Skill Passport → Career Profile → CV pipeline. Self-report and goal selection never award skills.
