# Connected Promotion Assessment and Bank Reconciliation

## Delivered

The first promotion review is now a playable, bilingual connected control file rather than a descriptive placeholder. The learner reviews four supporting documents, handles an AP quantity mismatch, identifies a supported customer transfer, classifies five bank-reconciliation differences, proves one adjusted balance, and chooses a documented close action.

The bank statement starts at 256,900 and the bank ledger at 235,400. Correct treatment of the deposit in transit, outstanding cheques, bank fee, interest, and supported customer transfer brings both sides to 250,400. The engine scores accuracy, accounting judgment, investigation, risk awareness, independence, and documentation. Unsupported posting and forcing a ledger balance are explicit critical control errors.

## Progression and evidence

- Eligibility still requires a completed First Shift plus practiced document-analysis and journal-entry evidence.
- A passing result unlocks Delta Commerce through the existing company-tier rule.
- The best local attempt is projected to Skill Passport for document analysis, journal entries, and bank reconciliation.
- Evidence is labeled local simulation evidence. It never becomes `Verified`, never represents employment, and does not overwrite a stronger prior attempt.
- A later failed retry cannot revoke an already-earned pass.
